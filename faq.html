Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PolyArticle — the personalized news platform designed to help you stay informed without overwhelming noise.

What is PolyArticle?

PolyArticle is a personalized news platform that delivers important stories through a swipe-card interface. Instead of endless scrolling, you receive a curated set of relevant articles tailored to your interests.

How does PolyArticle personalize my news feed?

PolyArticle adapts your feed based on the topics you select and your interactions with articles, such as swiping, saving, or opening stories.

Where does the news content come from?

PolyArticle aggregates articles from reputable publishers such as Reuters, The Guardian, Financial Times, MIT Technology Review, and other trusted news organizations.

Does PolyArticle create its own news articles?

No. PolyArticle does not produce original news reporting. The platform aggregates and organizes articles from established publishers and directs readers to the original sources.

Is PolyArticle free to use?

Yes. The core experience of PolyArticle is free. Optional premium features may be introduced in the future.

How does the swipe interface work?

Articles are presented as cards. You can swipe left to skip an article, swipe right to save it, or tap the card to read the full story from the original publisher.

Does PolyArticle collect personal data?

PolyArticle only collects minimal data such as your email and interaction signals needed to personalize your feed. For full details please see the Privacy Policy.

Is PolyArticle affiliated with news publishers?

No. PolyArticle is an independent platform that aggregates publicly available news articles and links readers to the original publishers.

How can I contact the PolyArticle team?

You can reach us anytime at hello@polyarticle.com.

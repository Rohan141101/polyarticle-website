At PolyArticle, your privacy is foundational. PolyArticle aggregates publicly available news articles from trusted publishers and directs users to the original sources for full reading. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information required to operate the service and personalize your news experience.

Email address — Used for account creation and login.

Interaction signals — Swipes, saved articles, and topic preferences used to personalize your news feed.

We do not collect location data, device contacts, microphone input, or camera access.

How We Use Information

Your data is used exclusively to operate and improve the PolyArticle service.

• Authenticating your account and maintaining login sessions.

• Improving article recommendations and personalization.

• Maintaining service stability and product performance.

We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Third-Party Services

PolyArticle relies on a limited set of trusted third-party services for infrastructure and content delivery.

Examples may include:

• Cloud hosting providers • Authentication providers • Licensed news aggregation APIs

These services process only the minimum data necessary to operate the platform.

Data Protection

PolyArticle implements modern security practices to protect user information.

• Encryption in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

• Encrypted storage for sensitive account data.

• Access restrictions and monitoring for backend infrastructure.

We continuously review security practices to maintain high standards of protection.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the PolyArticle service.

If you delete your account or request removal, associated personal data will be deleted from our systems within a reasonable time period.

Children's Privacy

PolyArticle is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we learn that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected, it will be deleted immediately.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under privacy laws such as GDPR or CCPA.

These may include:

• Access to your personal data • Correction of inaccurate information • Deletion of your data • Data portability

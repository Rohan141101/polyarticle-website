Our Story

Built for the
informed human

PolyArticle is a personalized news platform built to help people stay informed without information overload. Instead of endless scrolling, PolyArticle presents a curated deck of important stories from trusted publishers.

A calmer way to read the news. Relevant stories. Trusted sources. No noise.

Our Mission

Stay informed. Stay in control.

The modern news ecosystem is overwhelming. Thousands of articles are published every hour, yet readers often struggle to separate signal from noise.

PolyArticle was created to rethink how people discover news. Instead of infinite scrolling and algorithmic chaos, PolyArticle delivers a curated and finite deck of stories tailored to your interests.

Our goal is simple: help people understand the world without overwhelming them with information.

The Problem

Why modern news feels overwhelming

Today’s news platforms compete for attention rather than clarity. Endless feeds, breaking alerts, and viral headlines create a constant stream of information that often leaves readers feeling more anxious than informed.

PolyArticle approaches news differently. Instead of maximizing engagement, we focus on helping readers discover meaningful stories quickly and calmly.

How We Curate

Trusted journalism, organized for you

PolyArticle aggregates stories from respected publishers across topics like technology, science, climate, finance, and global affairs.

Our platform prioritizes editorial quality and personal relevance. Instead of showing every article published online, PolyArticle surfaces stories that are most meaningful based on your interests and interactions.

When you open a story, you are taken directly to the original publisher so journalism continues to be supported at its source.

A new way to read the news

PolyArticle combines trusted journalism with a modern discovery experience. By presenting articles through a swipe-based interface and personalized feed, readers can explore global news without the friction of traditional news apps.

The platform functions as both a news reader and a news aggregator, bringing together articles from established publishers in one clean and focused environment.
